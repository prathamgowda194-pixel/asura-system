Structural Gastronomy

The Anatomy of a Super Cone

Engineered across four micro-calibrated architectural strata for an uncompromised sensory gradient from first peak to final bite.

STRATUM 01

Botanical Crown

Hand-sliced Mediterranean figs, edible 24k Bavarian gold leaf flakes, and cold-pressed fruit essence drizzle that releases volatile aromatics upon touch.

STRATUM 02

Velvet Sub-Zero Swirl

A2 organic Jersey cream sub-zero churned at -7°C to achieve a microscopic air-cell structure, eliminating ice crystallization for pure silk mouthfeel.

STRATUM 03

Compote Molten Core

Slow-reduced wild fruit compote poured into the central column of the cone at 4°C, providing a tart, juicy burst midway through the scoop.

STRATUM 04

Charcoal Waffle Hull

Activated coconut shell charcoal blended into French butter waffle batter, double-pressed in custom diamond cast-iron plates for acoustic crunch.
