Architectural Anatomy

Rigorous structural engineering meets tactile material provenance. The buildings are conceived as extensions of the Cap Martin promontory rather than additions upon it.

01 / Materiality

Tivoli Silver Travertine

Monolithic 80mm vein-cut slabs quarried in Lazio, fluted with robotic CNC micro-grooves to create subtle acoustic diffusion and rhythmic shadow lines.

02 / Engineering

Post-Tensioned Concrete

Self-consolidating 40MPa architectural concrete with rough-sawn Douglas fir board formwork, enabling cantilever spans up to 16 metres with zero external propping.

03 / Envelope

Starphire Glass Envelope

3.8-metre floor-to-ceiling ultra-clear low-iron acoustic laminates with motorized concealed floor tracks, achieving an acoustic insulation index of Rw 48dB.

04 / Environmental

Geothermal & Solar Core

Deep closed-loop geothermal heat exchange coupled with integrated 48kW monocrystalline solar roof arrays, providing 100% net-zero annual energy balance.
