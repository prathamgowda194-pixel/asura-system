Where minimalist structural clarity meets the endless horizon.

Carlouel Beach House represents an unmatched synthesis of modern tropical minimalism and architectural permanence. Positioned on an expansive stretch of white sand along the Gulf Coast, the residence was engineered around a single design ethos: uninterrupted light.

Floor-to-ceiling floor-plate glass panels slide seamlessly into concealed wall pockets, expanding the living space out onto wide travertine loggias and an edge-free infinity pool reflecting the turquoise Gulf waters.
