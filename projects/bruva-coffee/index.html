THE COORG HIGHLANDS

Shade-grown under
ancient rosewood trees.

In our ancestral plantation nestled within the Western Ghats of Karnataka, coffee is not forced. It ripens slowly under towering fig and jackfruit canopies, nurtured by rich black volcanic soil and morning mountain dew.

We pick only the deepest ruby-red cherries by hand. Sun-dried on raised bamboo beds and woodfire-roasted in small 12kg batches to preserve the delicate essential oils.
