Questions & Guidance

Quietly answered.

Why is the serum naturally sky-blue?

The clear skyish-azure tone comes purely from natural chamazulene molecules formed during steam distillation of golden Tanacetum annuum flowers. We never add artificial pigments or FD&C colorants.

Where does the morning/night ritual fit in a routine?

Apply three drops (0.15 ml) onto slightly damp cleansed skin prior to any heavier facial oils or mineral SPF. The tri-weight hyaluron uses ambient surface moisture to lock in supple elasticity.

Is the DIN 18 flint glass dropper refillable?

Yes. Both the 30 ml and 50 ml flint glass bottles share standard DIN 18/410 European neck threading, allowing infinite reuse of the anodized aluminum and glass pipette collar.
