PARENTS COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

What syllabus and classes do you coach?
We provide tailored academic tuition for Classes 1 through 10 across CBSE, ICSE, and Karnataka State syllabi. Key subjects include Mathematics, Science (Physics, Chemistry, Biology), English, Social Science, and regional languages.
How are child psychological counseling sessions structured?
Our sessions are strictly 1-on-1 and confidential. We conduct a preliminary diagnostic assessment with both parent and child, followed by customized behavioral therapy, attention-focus exercises, and stress coping techniques.
What is the batch size for tuition classes?
We maintain strictly small cohorts of 8 to 12 students per batch to ensure each child receives personal attention, doubts clarification, and individualized homework tracking.
Where is the centre physically located?
We are located at: Aadriti Learning Centre, 902, 9th Cross Rd, 2nd Phase, Gokula 1st Stage, Divanarapalya, Yeswanthpur, Bengaluru, Karnataka 560054. Safe parking and accessible pickup bays are available.
