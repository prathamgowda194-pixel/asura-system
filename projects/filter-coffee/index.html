SAME POUR

Served in brass, as it always was.

Tumbler and dabara, too hot to hold. It is how filter coffee has always been served, and how we have served it for twenty-five years.

First, a slow tilt.

The tumbler is raised high above the dabara. The height is the craft: the longer the fall, the finer the froth.

Then the metre pour.

One unbroken ribbon from tumbler to dabara. It cools the cup, marries milk and decoction, and wakes the aroma.

Froth, and then silence.

A thick, bubbled crown settles on top. No machine and no steam wand. Only gravity, brass, and practice.
